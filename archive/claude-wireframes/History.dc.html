Why the rules come in layers

E-Verify started as a small federal pilot. Contracts and states added their own rules over time, so one business can have federal, contract, and state rules at once.

YearWhat happened
1996Basic Pilot program created; legislation authored by Rep. Ken Calvert
2004Available to employers in all 50 states
2007Renamed E-Verify
2008Arizona requires it for all employers; executive order for federal contractors
2009Contractor clause (FAR 52.222-54) takes effect
2016California limits when employers can use it, Labor Code 2814
TodayVoluntary for most employers; required for some by contract or state law
