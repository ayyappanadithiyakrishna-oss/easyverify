E-Verify each new hire, only after the job offer is accepted

Federal + California (2 rules combined)

What it means

Your federal subcontract requires E-Verify for new hires. California does not allow E-Verify checks before a job offer. So: check after the offer is accepted and the I-9 is done.

Why it applies to you

  • Federal subcontract: yes
  • E-Verify clause in contract: yes
  • State: California

What to do

  1. Job offer accepted
  2. Complete Form I-9
  3. Create the E-Verify case
  4. Give the employee any E-Verify notice

Sources

FAR 52.222-54 · CA Labor Code 2814
