**No interviews have been done.** Do not describe any in the submission until rows below are real. Get permission before naming anyone or their business; "owner of a small restaurant in Riverside" is enough.

## Who to ask (3 to 5 people)

Owners or managers of businesses with under 50 employees who have hired someone in the last two years. Good places: family and neighbors with businesses, school-community contacts, a local chamber of commerce. Do not ask anyone to share employee records.

## 10-minute script

1. Tell me about the last person you hired. What paperwork did you do on their first week?
2. Which part was confusing or took the longest?
3. Have you heard of E-Verify? Is your business required to use it? How did you find out?
4. Where do you look when you have a question about hiring rules?
5. (Show the plan page, `/plan`.) Without my help, what do you think this tells you? What is missing or unclear?
6. What would make you trust or not trust a tool like this?
7. Would you use it? What would you want it to do that it doesn't?

Take notes on what they *said* and *did*, not what you hoped.
