# EasyVerify

## Register
product

## Users
Inland Empire small-business employers organizing employment-verification steps, and invited new hires completing their own responsibilities.

## Product Purpose
Explain applicable requirements with official sources, explicit unknowns, a working calendar, and separate employer/new-hire confirmations. Demonstrate the underlying programming through the rules engine and profile comparison.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, practical. The user supplied Cursor as the visual reference and requested a warmer, more distinctive site with dark mode.

## Anti-references
Generic white SaaS card grids; decorative AI effects; invented compliance scores; nonworking authentication buttons.

## Design Principles
Make the next step visible. Explain why each requirement applies. Show uncertainty at the point of decision. Preserve sample/real workspace boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile layouts, reduced motion, and an explicit light/dark preference.
